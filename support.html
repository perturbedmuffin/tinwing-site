Tinwing Support

Get help

Found a bug, have a question, or need help with a purchase? Open an issue at github.com/perturbedmuffin/tinwing-site/issues and include your iPhone model and what happened.

Purchases

Purchases are handled by Apple. To restore No Ads or the Supporter Pack on a new phone, open the Shop in the Hangar and tap Restore Purchases. For refunds, use reportaproblem.apple.com.

Ads and tracking

You can turn ad tracking off any time in iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. See the Privacy Policy.
