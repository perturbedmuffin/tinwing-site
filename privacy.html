Tinwing Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026

Tinwing is a game for iPhone made by an independent developer ("we"). This policy explains what information is used when you play. We run no servers that the game talks to, and there are no accounts, no chat and no analytics of our own.

What stays on your phone

Your progress is saved only on your iPhone: your planes, coins, faction XP, Flight School badges, your call sign (a name the game makes from two word lists, like "Secretive Impala"), the map you picked, your settings, and what you bought in the game. We never see it. Deleting the app deletes it.

Playing online with Game Center

Online play uses Game Center, Apple's own service. If you are signed in to Game Center:

  • Apple finds other players for a match, delivers invites and lobby codes, and connects the phones in a match. That is between you and Apple, under Apple's privacy policy (apple.com/legal/privacy).
  • During a match the phones send each other only what the game needs to play: controls and plane positions. Nobody stores it.
  • The game shows your Game Center friends by their Game Center names, if you allow it (iOS asks the first time you open the friends sheet). Everyone else appears under a generated call sign. There is no chat of any kind.
  • Without Game Center the game plays fully offline against computer pilots.

Sharing a lobby code

When you tap Share, the iPhone's share sheet opens with a fixed message: the game's name, a four-digit code and a link that opens Tinwing. You choose who gets it and through which app; the game itself sends nothing.

Ads

Tinwing shows ads from Google AdMob to stay free: short videos you can choose to watch for coins ("Double it!" after a battle, "Free coins" in the Hangar), and sometimes a full-screen ad between battles (never during a battle, never in Flight School or a friends' party). There are no banner ads.

To show and measure ads, Google may collect information from your device, such as:

  • the advertising identifier (IDFA), only if you tap "Allow" when your iPhone asks about tracking;
  • approximate location (from your internet address);
  • how you interact with ads (for example, which ad was shown and whether it was tapped);
  • diagnostic and performance data (for example, app crashes and loading times).

If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", ads are not personalized. You can change this any time in iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

If you live where privacy law asks for consent to ads (GDPR regions), Google's consent form asks for your choice before ads are shown. You can change it later with Ad choices in the game's Shop.

How Google uses this information: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Purchases

In-app purchases (No Ads, the Supporter Pack, coin packs) are handled entirely by Apple. We never see your payment details. The game only remembers on your phone what you bought.

Children

Tinwing is for teens and up (App Store age rating 13+) and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices

  • Turn tracking off: iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Buy No Ads to stop the ads between battles (the videos you choose to watch stay).
  • Play without Game Center: the game plays fully offline.
  • Delete the app to delete everything the game saved on your phone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date before the change reaches the game.
